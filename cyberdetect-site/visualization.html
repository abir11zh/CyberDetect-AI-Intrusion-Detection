What the Data Tells Us

DDoS Peak at 11:00

The traffic spike visible at 11:00 corresponds to a simulated DDoS burst reaching 1 250 packets/s — nearly 1.4× the normal business-hours baseline.

DDoS Dominates Attacks

DDoS accounts for 28 % of all detected attack events, making it the most prevalent threat, followed by Port Scan (18 %) and Brute Force (14 %).

128–256 B Most Common

The 128–256 byte packet-size bin has the highest volume (4 920 packets), consistent with typical HTTP/S request header sizes in legitimate traffic.
